Terms of Service

What the app is

AutoClipper Personale is a private, non-commercial tool run by its owner on his own computer. It turns long videos and stream recordings into short vertical clips with subtitles and sends them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Use of TikTok

The app only uploads drafts to the inbox of the TikTok account that authorized it. Nothing is published automatically: every draft is reviewed, edited and published by the account owner inside the TikTok app. Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Content

The owner only processes videos he has the right to use, such as his own recordings or content whose creators allow clips, and is responsible for what he publishes.

No warranty

The app is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings.
